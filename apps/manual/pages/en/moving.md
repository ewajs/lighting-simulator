# Moving

## Dragging

Grab a luminaire and drag it. It slides along the surface it's mounted on:
- ceiling luminaires along the ceiling;
- floor lamps along the floor;
- pendants at their height;
- wall lights along the walls, even from one wall to another.

With several selected, they move together. Each drag undoes in one go with
**⌘Z**.

![Dragging a luminaire: a blue alignment guide and distances to the walls](/screenshots/en/dragging.png)

**While you drag:**
- **Blue lines with measurements** show the distance to the walls and, for
  wall lights, the height above the floor.
- **A dashed blue line** appears when you line up with another luminaire or
  with the middle of the room.

## The arrows

With the **Move** tool (key **W**), the selection shows arrows. Drag an arrow
to move **in one direction only**:
- **red:** side to side;
- **blue:** front to back;
- **green:** height (pendants and wall lights only).

![The Move arrows on a pendant](/screenshots/en/move-arrows.png)

## The magnet

With the **Magnet** on, which is how it starts:
- positions land on a **10 cm grid**, measured from the middle of the room;
- they **line up** with other luminaires and with the middle of the room;
- turning goes in **15°** steps.

To move freely, hold **Alt** (⌥ on a Mac) while dragging, or switch the
Magnet off.

## Exact values

In **Properties → Position** you can type **X**, **Height** and **Z** in
metres. X and Z are measured from the middle of the room. The keyboard's
up and down arrows change them in 5 cm steps.
