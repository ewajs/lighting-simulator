# The view

## Getting around the scene

| To… | With a mouse | On a touch screen |
|---|---|---|
| Orbit | Drag on empty space | One finger on empty space |
| Pan | Right-drag | Two fingers |
| Zoom | The wheel | Pinch |

## Views and modes

![Plan view, in clay mode](/screenshots/en/views.png)

- **3D, Plan, Front, Side:** ready-made views (keys **0**, **7**, **8**, **9**).
  The button below them, **Frame selection** (key **F**), brings the camera
  to what you've selected.
- **Realistic** shows the room's colours. **Clay** paints everything a
  neutral grey, so you only look at the light (keys **1** and **2**).
  **Illuminance** is coming later.
- **Exposure:** brightens or darkens the image, like a camera, without
  changing the luminaires. Double-click resets it to 0.
