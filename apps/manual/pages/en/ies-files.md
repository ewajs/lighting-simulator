# Your IES files

An **IES file** describes how a luminaire spreads its light. Manufacturers
publish them with each product. You can try your own: at the bottom of the
catalog, under **Try your own IES file**, drop the file or tap **Load IES
file…**.

![The form for adding an IES file](/screenshots/en/ies-import.png)

Check what Lux Studio suggests:
- **Name:** taken from the file.
- **Mount type:** recessed, pendant, track… It decides where the luminaire goes.
- **Colour temperature:** 3000 K when the file doesn't say.

Tap **Add to catalog**. The product appears first, under **Your IES files**,
ready to use.

It's for this visit only: reloading the page removes it. If the file isn't a
valid IES file, Lux Studio tells you why.
