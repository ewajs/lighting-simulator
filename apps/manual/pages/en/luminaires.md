# Adding and picking luminaires

## The catalog

![The catalog with categories, search and filters](/screenshots/en/catalog.png)

- **Search** by name; accents don't matter.
- **Filter** by **Mount** (recessed, pendant, track, wall, floor or table)
  and by colour **Temperature**, or tap a category.
- Each product shows its **power, colour temperature and flux** (lumens).

**To add a luminaire:**
- **click** the product, and it appears in the middle of the room;
- or **drag** it into the 3D view, and it lands where you drop it.

Each kind goes where it belongs: recessed and track luminaires on the
ceiling, wall lights on a wall, floor lamps on the floor, and pendants at
their suspension height.

## Picking

| To… | Do this |
|---|---|
| Pick one | Click the luminaire |
| Add one to, or remove it from, the selection | **Shift**-click |
| Pick them all | **⌘A** (or **Ctrl+A**) |
| Pick none | Click empty space, or **Esc** |

Thin luminaires, such as a linear profile, are picked even if your click
lands a little to the side.

## The scene

The **Scene** tab lists everything you've placed, with the scene's totals:
number of luminaires, power, flux and power per square metre. You can pick
from the list too: click, or **Shift/⌘**-click.

![The scene list with totals](/screenshots/en/scene-list.png)

## Duplicate, switch off, delete

With something selected, in **Properties**:
- ⏻ **switches it on or off** (shortcut **H**);
- **Duplicate** makes a copy half a metre to the side (shortcut **⌘D**);
- 🗑 **deletes** it (shortcut **Delete**).

With several selected, **Dimming** and these buttons apply to all of them.
