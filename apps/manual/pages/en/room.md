# The room

With nothing selected, **Properties** shows the room.

![The room's properties: size and surfaces](/screenshots/en/room-inspector.png)

- **Dimensions:** width, depth and height in metres. Luminaires and furniture that end up
  outside move back in by themselves.
- **Floor, walls and ceiling:** each surface has a **colour** and a
  **reflectance ρ**, the share of light it sends back. A white wall returns
  about 80 %, concrete 40 % and a dark floor 10 %. The buttons (White paint,
  Warm grey, Concrete, Oak, Charcoal) set typical values.
- **Material:** instead of a colour, a surface can take a material, such as
  oak planks, tiles or red brick. Each is offered only where it makes sense:
  oak for the floor, brick for the walls. Picking one sets the reflectance to
  its typical value, and the slider still makes it lighter or darker. To go
  back to a colour, pick one of the colours.

A material's brightness follows its reflectance, not how bright the photo
was. So what you see matches how much light the surface sends back.

Reflectance changes how much light bounces around the room. That's why a dark
room looks dimmer with the same luminaires.
