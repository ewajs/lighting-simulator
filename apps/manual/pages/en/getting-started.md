# Getting started

Lux Studio lets you lay out a room, furnish it, place luminaires from a
catalog and see how it's lit. It all runs in the browser: there's nothing to
install, and the scene saves itself as you work.

## The screen

![The Lux Studio screen with its five areas numbered](/screenshots/en/overview.png)

1. **Catalog:** the luminaires and, with the switch at its top, the
   furniture you can add. Next to it, the **Scene** tab lists what you've
   placed, and **Analysis** calculates how much light reaches each surface.
2. **3D view:** the room. This is where you pick, move and aim luminaires
   and furniture.
3. **Properties:** whatever is selected, or the room when nothing is.
4. **Top bar:** undo and redo; **New**, **Open**, **Save** and **Share**;
   screenshot, panels, theme, language and **?**, which opens a quick
   getting-started guide.
5. **Tools:** **Move**, **Aim** and **Magnet**. How the scene is drawn is at
   the top centre, the camera views on the right, and exposure at the bottom.

## In a minute

1. Click a luminaire in the catalog: it appears in the middle of the room.
2. Drag it in the 3D view to where you want it.
3. In **Properties**, lower its **Dimming** or change its **Colour temperature**.
4. Made a mistake? **⌘Z** (or **Ctrl+Z**) undoes it.

Want to start from another room? **New** opens an empty room and
ready-made templates: see [Saving, opening and sharing](./saving).
