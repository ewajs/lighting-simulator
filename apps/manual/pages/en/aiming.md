# Aiming

**Recessed** and **track** luminaires can be aimed. Pick one and switch to
the **Aim** tool (key **E**).

![Aiming a spot: where the light lands, and the turning ring](/screenshots/en/aiming.png)

- **The dot** marks where the middle of the beam lands, on the floor or a
  wall. Drag it to where you want the light, such as a painting, and the
  luminaire turns to point there. Tilt goes up to 90°, level.
- **The ring** around the luminaire turns it in place (its **pan**) without
  changing the tilt. That's for asymmetric luminaires, like a wall washer
  that throws light to one side. With the Magnet on, it turns in 15° steps.

You can also type **Tilt** and **Pan** in **Properties → Aim**. To move
things again, pick **Move** or press **W**.
