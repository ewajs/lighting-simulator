# Adjusting the light

![A luminaire's properties](/screenshots/en/luminaire-inspector.png)

- **Dimming:** how much light it gives, from 0 to 100 %.
- **Colour temperature:** from 2200 K, warm, to 6500 K, cool. It starts at the
  product's own.
- **⏻:** switches it on or off without losing its dimming.
- **Product:** power, luminous flux, nominal colour temperature and beam
  angle, as the manufacturer states them.

## Luminaires with an IES file

Products with an **IES file** (in the demo, the ones marked "(IES)") use the
real light distribution the manufacturer measured: where the light goes,
how much goes up, whether it's symmetric. The others are estimated from
their flux and beam angle.

## Shadows

The **8 luminaires that give the most light** cast shadows. The rest still
light the room, without shadows, so everything stays smooth.
