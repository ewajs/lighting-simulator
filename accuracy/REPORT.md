# Accuracy: Lux Studio against Radiance

Generated by `npm run accuracy:report` from `accuracy/references` (ADR 0058, 0060). As
installed (no maintenance factor). "Direct" is the light straight from the luminaires;
"total" adds every bounce off the room's surfaces. "Bright" points have at least
10 % of the room's brightest point; "near" ones are within 1 m of a luminaire.
